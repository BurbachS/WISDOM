Abstract

The Wearable Integrated Stress Data Ontology Model (WISDOM) is an ontology for the semantic representation of wearable-based stress monitoring experiments. It integrates concepts related to stressors, stress conditions, physiological and behavioral responses, experimental activities, wearable sensors, physiological measurements, and annotation data into a unified knowledge model. WISDOM builds upon established Semantic Web standards, including SOSA/SSN, OWL-Time, QUDT, and Schema.org, to promote interoperability and the integration of heterogeneous stress datasets. The ontology supports the semantic annotation, integration, and analysis of multimodal stress data collected from wearable devices and complementary laboratory measurements.
